1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Bright Byte Lab ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information from you when you use the Grammar AI Check – Writing assistant & Keyboard support ( GramrAI Application ) mobile application (the "App").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information, which may include Personal Information:

  • User Identifiers & Authentication Data: When you create an account, we collect a unique Firebase User ID. This ID allows you to access and save your data. We do not directly collect or store your passwords (they are managed securely by Firebase).
  • AI Content Data (Prompts and Generations): The text prompts, inputs, and the resulting AI-generated content (text, code, or images) that you submit or create within the App. This data is essential for the App's core functionality.
  • Usage Data & Analytics: Information about how you access and use the App, such as features used, date and time of visits, device type, operating system version, and crash reports. This helps us improve stability.
  • Purchase & Subscription Data: Information related to your in-app purchases (IAP) and subscription status. We receive anonymized purchase receipts and subscription status identifiers, but we do not process or store your full credit card information.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for various purposes:

  • To manage your account, track subscription access, and process payments (via third-party processors).
  • To analyze usage and performance data to monitor and improve the App's quality, functionality, and stability.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues or security concerns.
  • To communicate with you about your account, updates, or changes to the policy.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Disclosure of Your Information (Third-Party Services)

We rely on trusted third-party services to operate and maintain the App. Your information may be shared with these services only as necessary to perform their functions:

  • Firebase (Google): Used for secure user authentication, database storage (if applicable, e.g., saving user history), and crash/performance analytics.
  • RevenueCat: Used to manage and validate your in-app purchases and subscription entitlements. RevenueCat processes payment data on our behalf but does not store or access your full payment details.
  • Generative AI Providers (e.g., Gemini API via Firebase AI Gen): Your prompts and requests are securely transmitted to the generative AI service provider to process your request and generate content.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., a court or government agency).

5. Data Retention and Security

  • Retention: We retain your Personal Information only for as long as necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy, typically until you delete your account.
